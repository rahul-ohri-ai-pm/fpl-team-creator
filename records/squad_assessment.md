## Identified Gaps & Data Limitations

### Gap: New Signing Integration (João Pedro, Thiago)
- **Problem:** João Pedro (Club Brugge → Chelsea) has no PL track record. xGI/points_per_game are PL-only fields.
- **How it's handled:** Price-fitted baseline (3.53 ppg) + preseason friendly minutes reliability blending (304/450 sourced).
- **Actual input to model:** Predicted 4.81 (xGI blend added ~0.28, reliability blending added ~0.5). Still ranks 73/262 MID, not a top-tier pick.
- **Risk:** If João Pedro is a breakout performer (e.g., pens or right-place-right-time returns), model will systematically under-predict him until PL data accumulates.
- **Mitigation:** Captain the choice on Mbeumo/Enzo if both perform; bench João Pedro until he scores 2+ times to validate. GW4-5 re-run will auto-tune once PL points land.

### Gap: Preseason Minutes (Stach, others from promoted clubs)
- **Problem:** Leeds and Sunderland friendly minutes not sourced (behind paywall). Stach's reliability score is capped at 0.55 (unknown baseline).
- **How it's handled:** Treated as unknown (UNKNOWN_RELIABILITY 0.55); expected to rise once League games start accruing form.
- **Actual impact:** Stach 4.40 predicted is low tier; if he's a nailed starter and plays 90 min vs Nottm Forest GW1, he could bank 5-6 easily.
- **Mitigation:** After GW1-2, Stach's form will update in bootstrap. If he outperforms, GW3 will see automatic promotion.

### Gap: Injury Recovery Confidence (Mukiele 75%)
- **Problem:** Mukiele flagged 75% available by FPL (knee knock, pre-season). Model carries no injury-decay weighting—he's scored as full-value (5.04).
- **Actual risk:** 25% chance he's benched or plays <60 min vs Ipswich → real points <3. Predicted 5.04 assumes starts.
- **Mitigation:** Monitor team news Thu-Fri before GW1 finishes. If Mukiele is benched, swap for Tarkowski (£4.5m, ready on bench) same day.

### Gap: Set-Piece Participation (Gabriel, Virgil, others)
- **Problem:** Points_per_game and xGI don't separate set-piece returns (corners, throw-ins) from open-play. Model treats all returns equally.
- **Actual impact:** Defenders who take pens or FKs (Virgil for Liverpool corners, Gabriel for corners) can have +0.5–1.0 variance in any given week.
- **Mitigation:** Captain choice on penalty-takers on fixtures with weak opposition (Gabriel vs Coventry was ideal, but 0-0 meant no opportunity). Monitor FPL news for corner-kick allocation changes.
